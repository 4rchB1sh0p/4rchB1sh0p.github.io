# Biography

Yashodhar Mahajan describes himself as an automation engineer and software developer on a multidisciplinary journey. His technical path began in mechanical engineering, especially around cars, factories, gears, designs, and production machinery. That foundation later expanded into software development, programming, controls, and automated systems.

His profile is built around the combination of old and new engineering skills: mechanical design, machine understanding, programming, industrial controls, and software systems. The website frames this as a curiosity-led shift from traditional mechanical engineering into code-driven automation.

Yashodhar's experience has taken him through labs, schools, and businesses. Across those environments, he emphasizes learning from different kinds of problems and developing the ability to think at both system level and detail level. This combination is presented as central to how he approaches technical work.

Outside professional engineering, Yashodhar also writes and participates in community-oriented learning. The site says he helps children learn about technology through charity work, and his blog references documentation and social-work experiences with Bodh Shiksha Samiti, an NGO focused on education for underprivileged children in Rajasthan.

## Narrative Themes

- Mechanical engineer who moved into software and automation.
- Technologist comfortable crossing domains instead of staying inside one narrow discipline.
- Practical builder interested in automated systems, testing, machines, and production environments.
- Learner shaped by labs, schools, companies, and volunteer work.
- Writer interested in growth, self-reflection, documentation, and life abroad.

## Profile Tone

The portfolio uses a conversational tone. It presents Yashodhar as curious, hands-on, playful, technically ambitious, and interested in making technology useful beyond formal engineering workplaces.
